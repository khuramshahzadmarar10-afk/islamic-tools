    Common questions

    Are the prayer times exact?

    They are astronomical estimates using Muslim World League angles and standard Asr. Mosque schedules can use different conventions, safety offsets, or local observations, so compare with your trusted local timetable.

    Why can a Hijri date differ by one day?

    The Islamic calendar follows lunar months. Countries and communities may begin a month based on different moon-sighting decisions, while digital calendars use a calculated calendar.

    Does the Qibla tool store my location?

    No. If you allow browser location access, the coordinates are used only on this page to calculate a bearing to the Kaaba. They are not saved or sent by the tool.

    What usage data is collected?

    Your prayer, Qibla, date, and Zakat inputs remain in your browser and are not sent by the tools. Google Analytics does receive website usage data, such as page visits and device or browser information, to help measure how the site is used.

    Which nisab should I choose for Zakat?

    Scholarly guidance varies on whether to use the gold or silver threshold in a given situation. Ask a qualified scholar familiar with your circumstances if you are unsure.

    Is the Ramadan start date confirmed?

    No. It is a calculated estimate for planning. The confirmed first day can vary by location and is normally announced after moon sighting.
