Zakat Nisab & When Zakat Becomes Due

Understand the Zakat nisab for gold and silver, full ownership, the lunar-year condition and the standard 2.5% rate before using the Zakat calculator.

References: Quran 9:60 for recipient categories; widely used nisab standards of 85 g gold or 595 g silver. Schools of law differ on some details.

Gold nisab

85 g of gold. Multiply 85 by your current local gold price per gram.

Silver nisab

595 g of silver. Multiply 595 by your current local silver price per gram.

About NoorPulse Islamic Tools

A bilingual collection for prayer, fasting, giving, pilgrimage and everyday remembrance.

Calculations are designed for convenient planning and should be checked against trusted local religious guidance.

NoorPulse brings practical Islamic tools together in one clear, bilingual resource for Muslims worldwide. Whether you are organizing daily worship, preparing for Ramadan, calculating charitable obligations or planning a pilgrimage, the page offers focused guidance without requiring an account. The collection includes prayer times, namaz timings, a Qibla direction finder, a Hijri calendar, a Ramadan calendar, a Zakat calculator, an Umrah guide, Sehri and Iftar information, a Tasbih counter and sourced daily duas in Arabic with English and Urdu meanings.

For salah planning, the prayer times today panel calculates Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha from a chosen city or current coordinates. The namaz time results also provide the basis for the Ramadan timetable, showing Sehri ending at Fajr and Iftar beginning at Maghrib. The Qibla direction finder calculates the bearing toward the Kaaba, while the Hijri calendar displays the Hijri date today and converts selected Gregorian dates into Islamic dates. Because calculation methods and local announcements can vary, NoorPulse clearly recommends following a trusted mosque or accepted authority where its results differ.

The Zakat calculator estimates eligible net wealth after liabilities and compares it with a gold- or silver-based nisab entered at a current local price. Supporting Zakat information explains ownership, the lunar-year condition and the widely used 2.5% rate. The Ramadan calendar and countdown help with advance planning, while the complete Umrah guide presents the essential sequence from Ihram and miqat through Tawaf, Sa‘i and completion. For daily remembrance, the session-only Tasbih counter and the collection of Quranic and Prophetic duas keep useful Islamic tools close at hand. NoorPulse is intended as a convenient planning resource, not a replacement for qualified scholarship or local official announcements.
